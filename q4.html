Quad Screener history (momentum tails)

How to read it: each dot is a stock or ETF placed by Volland's Quad Screener. Left↔right = how options dealers' vanna leans vs. its last 6 months (right = upside lean, left = downside lean). Top↔bottom = expected calm (top) vs. volatility (bottom). The faded tail behind each dot traces where it sat in earlier snapshots, so you can see which way it's drifting, like SpotGamma's Compass chart. A tail moving down-and-left is getting more bearish. The key comparison is the post-OPEX rerun on Mon Oct 19. Example question: does PLTR leave the green top-right box once Friday's vanna expires? Hollow rings = position approximate (only the quadrant is known). It's a simplified tool, and Volland says it shouldn't be the only basis for a trade.

Education only, not financial advice. "Theory trades" describe how a bearish view could be expressed; they are not recommendations. Prices from Robinhood market data; times are Central (CT).
